Welcome to **Leaf & Living**! A modern, responsive, and user-friendly e-commerce web application designed for plant lovers. Discover, filter, and buy your favorite indoor and outdoor plants with detailed plant-care guides to keep your greens healthy and thriving.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

- 🏠 **Engaging Home Page:** Hero banner, curated plant categories, and trending best-sellers.
- 🛍️ **Interactive Product Listing:** Filter plants by sunlight requirements, watering needs, and price range.
- 🪴 **Detailed Product View:** High-res gallery, pot size selector, and visual care indicator badges.
- 🛒 **Dynamic Shopping Cart:** Real-time subtotal calculation, quantity adjustments, and item removal.
- 💳 **Seamless Checkout Process:** User-friendly form with client-side validation and order summary.
- 📖 **Plant Care Guide:** Comprehensive tips on watering, lighting, and troubleshooting plant health.
- 📱 **Fully Responsive:** Smooth visual layout optimized across mobile, tablet, and desktop viewports.
